## 5.2.0

### Added

- `descriptionRaw` on items and on the feed: the description text with its original line breaks and spacing, trimmed at both ends. On items it comes from `<description>` only. On the feed it follows the same fallback chain as `description` (`<description>`, then `itunes:summary`, then subtitle / `itunes:subtitle`). `description` is unchanged and still collapses whitespace.
- `podverse-partytime/core`: a parser entry point with no Node-only imports (no `fs`,
  `node-fetch`, `dotenv`, `pino`, or `Buffer`) that runs under React Native and other bundlers.
  It exports parser functions and types and does not read environment variables.
- `setPartytimeLogger` and `setPartytimeLogLevel` to route parser logs to a custom sink or silence
  console output.
- `Phase4LiveStatus`, `Phase4PodcastLiveItem`, and `Phase4PodcastLiveItemItem` exports from both
  `podverse-partytime` and `podverse-partytime/core`.
- `exports` map entries in `package.json` for root and `./core`, while existing `dist/*` deep
  imports still resolve.

### Changed

- Empty/missing item guid warnings now go through the partytime logger sink instead of writing to
  `console.warn` directly.
