## 5.1.0

### Added

- Phase 8 `<podcast:image>` at channel, item, and liveItem level, exposed as a `podcastImage` array (href, plus optional alt, aspectRatio, width, height, type, and purpose).
- Phase 8 `<podcast:follow>` at channel level, pointing at a JSON document of follow links.

### Changed

- Item titles that look like numbers (`007`, `1.10`) stay text instead of being coerced to numbers.
- `he` upgraded from 1.2.0 to 2.0.0 for decoding entities in tag and attribute values. Parsed output shape is otherwise unchanged.

### Requirements

- Requires Node.js 22.12 or newer. Partytime is CommonJS and `he` 2.x is ESM-only, which needs Node's unflagged `require()` of ES modules. Maintained Node lines (22, 24) meet this.
